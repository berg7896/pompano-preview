Frequently asked questions

What counts as a dental emergency?

Common examples are a bad toothache, a broken or chipped tooth, a lost or broken crown, bridge or filling, and swelling or infection in the teeth or gums.

What should I do if I have a dental emergency?

Get treatment as soon as possible. Call Implant Center of Pompano Beach at 954-998-1252 and we'll find you the earliest time.

Can you handle emergency wisdom tooth removal?

Often yes, the same day. It takes about 45 minutes depending on how many teeth come out, and several sedation options are available. See wisdom tooth removal.

Can you pull teeth the same day?

Possibly, depending on the situation, and we can often give you a replacement option the same day. We also treat chipped, cracked and painful teeth. Request an appointment or call us.

Can I finance emergency treatment?

Yes. Apply online in about 30 seconds, get funded, and start treatment. Checking your options won't affect your credit score. Learn about financing.
