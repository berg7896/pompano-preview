All-on-4 dental implants in Pompano Beach and nearby Boca Raton, Fort Lauderdale and Lighthouse Point

All-on-X is a full-arch restoration: a complete row of teeth held by four to six dental implants. Unlike dentures, it stays fixed in your mouth. You brush it, you don't take it out.

The implants are placed where your jawbone is densest. That helps them hold firmly, can help preserve your bone and facial shape, and lets you chew with much more strength than a denture allows.

How does All-on-4 work?

  1. 1

    Consultation and planning

    X-rays and a 3D scan show your bone, so we can plan where each implant goes.

  2. 2

    Implant placement

    Four to six titanium implants are placed as artificial tooth roots.

  3. 3

    Teeth the same day

    In many cases a temporary fixed set of teeth is attached the day of surgery.

  4. 4

    Healing

    Over a few months the implants fuse with your jawbone (osseointegration).

  5. 5

    Final teeth

    Your permanent, custom arch is made in our lab and fitted.

Am I a good candidate for All-on-4?

Most people who are missing most or all of their teeth can be. Your consult and 3D scan will tell you for sure. These are the things we look at:

Tooth loss

Best for people missing most or all teeth in one or both arches, from gum disease, decay or injury.

Jawbone density

You need enough bone to hold the implants. Because of how they are angled, bone grafts are often not needed.

Oral health

Active gum disease or infection is treated first.

Overall health

Conditions like uncontrolled diabetes or autoimmune disorders can affect healing, so we review your health history.

Non-smokers

Smoking slows healing. We ask smokers to quit, or at least stop around the time of surgery.

Aftercare

You'll need to follow the post-surgery instructions and keep up good daily cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the All-on-X procedure take?

The implant surgery is often done in one appointment. The full process, through healing and your final teeth, takes several months.

Is the procedure painful?

You are numbed with local anesthesia, or you can be asleep under general anesthesia. Soreness afterward is usually managed well with medication. See sedation options.

How long do All-on-X implants last?

With good care the implants can last a lifetime. The teeth attached to them may need adjustment or replacement over the years.

What's the maintenance routine for All-on-X?

Brush and clean around the arch every day, and keep your regular dental checkups.

Can I eat all types of food with All-on-X implants?

Once you've healed, you can eat most foods. Avoid extremely hard or sticky items.
