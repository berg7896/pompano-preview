Professional teeth whitening and bleaching in Broward County

Pompano Beach's professional cosmetic dentists

Teeth yellow and stain over time from coffee, tea, cola, red wine, smoking, and foods rich in chromogens (strongly colored compounds). Professional whitening removes those stains and brightens your teeth.

There are three main routes: in-office bleaching, at-home kits from your dentist, and over-the-counter products. Our custom trays fit your teeth closely, so the gel stays where it should and works evenly.

Teeth whitening questions

How does professional teeth whitening work?

A custom tray that fits over your teeth is filled with whitening gel and worn for about 30 minutes to an hour, depending on the treatment.

Is professional teeth whitening safe?

Generally yes, when done by a qualified dental professional. Follow the instructions you're given and tell us right away if you feel discomfort.

How long does teeth whitening last?

It depends on your oral hygiene, diet and habits. Results generally last from several months to a few years.

Are there side effects?

Some people have temporary sensitivity or mild discomfort. It usually goes away within a few days.

How much does teeth whitening cost?

Our custom whitening tray and gel special is $59, with the consultation, panoramic X-ray and exam included.
