Flexible payment options: making dental implants and other services affordable

Dental care can be hard on a budget, especially for major work like implants or a full-arch restoration. Many people don't have dental insurance, and cost is one of the most common reasons treatment gets put off until a small problem becomes a bigger one.

Financing lets you start treatment now and spread the cost into monthly payments that fit your budget. You can see your likely offers before you commit, and checking doesn't touch your credit score.

Have PPO insurance? We accept most PPO plans and verify your benefits for you, so you know what's covered before you finance the rest.

Dental financing questions

How does financing work?

Instead of paying the full cost up front, you pay for your treatment over time through a loan or credit line from one of our financing partners.

How do I apply for a payment plan?

Apply directly on our financing partners' websites. It takes about 30 seconds, and our team can walk you through it by phone.

Is there a credit check?

Most partners use a soft check to show your options, which doesn't affect your credit score. Accepting a loan or opening an account may affect it.

Does financing carry interest?

It depends on the plan. Some offer 0% interest; others charge interest. Compare the options before you choose.

Is there an up-front payment?

Some partners require a down payment and some don't. You'll see it in each offer before you accept.

What if I have bad credit?

Some partners may still approve you. Pre-qualifying shouldn't affect your credit, but selecting and taking out a loan may.

How does pre-qualification work?

Many partners let you see the offers you're likely to get on their websites, before you formally apply.

How do payments work?

With your authorization, payments are automatically debited from the account you choose. Each partner's website has the details.

Ready to finance?

Call us or send a request, and we'll help you check your options and book your free consult.

What happens after I send my message?

  1. Our front desk reviews your information.
  2. A team member contacts you as soon as possible.
  3. We work with your schedule to set your appointment.
