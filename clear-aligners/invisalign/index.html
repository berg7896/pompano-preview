What Invisalign® can treat

Crossbite

Some upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth when you bite.

Crowding

Not enough room, so teeth overlap or twist.

Open bite

Upper and lower teeth don't meet when you close.

Overbite

Upper front teeth overlap the lower ones too much.

Spacing

Gaps between teeth.

Underbite

Lower teeth sit in front of the upper teeth.

Pompano Beach's clear aligner and orthodontic team

Invisalign® straightens teeth with a series of clear, removable aligners. They are more comfortable and less invasive than traditional braces, and you take them out to eat and to clean your teeth.

You switch to a new set every 1 to 2 weeks, and each set moves your teeth a little further. A typical treatment takes 12 to 18 months.

  1. 1. Initial consultation. We talk about your goals and whether Invisalign® fits.
  2. 2. 3D scan. A digital scan of your teeth, no messy impressions.
  3. 3. Your roadmap. A plan for moving your teeth step by step.
  4. 4. Custom aligners. Your aligners are 3D-printed to fit you.
  5. 5. Aligner series. A new set about every 2 weeks.
  6. 6. Check-ins. A visit about every 2 months to track progress.

Invisalign® questions

What is Invisalign®?

Nearly invisible clear plastic aligners that fit over your teeth. They work like braces, but you can take them out to eat and drink.

What are Invisalign® aligners made of?

Clear, medical-grade plastic, custom-made to fit your mouth and shift your teeth gradually.

What are the benefits of Invisalign®?

They are nearly invisible, removable, more comfortable than braces, easier to keep clean, and can cost less in the long run.

How often do I have to wear my aligners?

At least 22 hours a day.

What happens if I forget to wear them?

Put them back in as soon as you can. Leaving them out for long periods can significantly delay your treatment.
