3D Predict questions

Can I preview what my results will look like?

Yes. 3D Predict gives you a 3D video of how your teeth will move and the expected final result.

Do 3D Predict aligners hurt?

They shouldn't. Some people feel pressure or soreness for the first 1 to 3 days of each new set, which over-the-counter pain relievers usually handle.

How long does 3D Predict treatment take?

Wear your aligners at least 22 hours a day. Simple cases can take a few months; most take 1 to 2 years.

Why is treating the entire tooth important for alignment?

Planning the root as well as the visible crown can prevent complications, mid-course corrections and longer treatment.

How do I care for my 3D Predict aligners?

Rinse them in clear, cool water whenever you take them out. If needed, brush them gently with a soft toothbrush.
