Clear aligner questions

Which is right for me, Invisalign® or 3D Predict?

Both straighten teeth with clear, removable aligners. 3D Predict also plans the movement of your roots, not just what you can see. We'll recommend one after your consultation and smile assessment.

How many hours a day do I wear aligners?

At least 22 hours a day. Take them out to eat, drink anything other than water, and clean your teeth.

How long does treatment take?

Simple cases can take a few months. Invisalign® treatment typically takes 12 to 18 months, and most aligner cases finish within 1 to 2 years.

How much do clear aligners cost?

Invisalign® starts at $199 per month. 3D Predict pricing is given at your consultation. Financing is available for every credit level.
