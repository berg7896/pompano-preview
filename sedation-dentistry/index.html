What is sedation dentistry?

Sedation dentistry in Pompano Beach and nearby Boca Raton, Fort Lauderdale and Lighthouse Point

Sedation dentistry, sometimes called sleep dentistry, uses medication to help you relax. It ranges from minimal sedation, where you stay awake, to general anesthesia, where you are asleep. We offer oral sedation, light IV sedation, and moderate and deep sedation.

LIGHTEST

Understanding oral sedation

A pill taken by mouth. Good for anxiety or phobias, and for longer or more involved procedures. The medicine, dose and timing are matched to your procedure and how relaxed you want to be. It's easy, needle-free and generally safe.

DEEPER

Basics of IV sedation

Medication goes directly into a vein, so it works quickly and can be adjusted. You relax deeply while breathing on your own, recover quickly, and usually remember little of the procedure. You're monitored the whole time.

NUMBING

Dental anesthesia

Anesthesia blocks pain signals so the area being treated is numb. The type we use depends on the procedure, your health history and your preferences.

Procedures that may use sedation or anesthesia

  • Dental implants
  • Tooth extractions
  • Root canals
  • Cavity fillings

Frequently asked questions

Does dental insurance cover sedation dentistry?

Most plans don't, but check with your provider. If the procedure is medically necessary, sedation may be partly covered. We'll verify your benefits for you.

What is the cost of sedation dentistry?

It depends on the type of sedation. Sedation starts from about $500 [CONFIRM]. Our team will go over the cost and your budget with you before treatment.

Is sedation dentistry safe?

Yes. Your vital signs are monitored throughout, and most patients have minimal side effects.

How long after receiving sedation can you drive a car?

Don't drive for 12 to 24 hours, depending on the type of sedation. Plan for someone to take you home. We'll go over all precautions with you.
