Veneer questions

What are dental veneers?

Very thin pieces of porcelain or another material placed over the front of your teeth. They can change a tooth's color, shape, size or length, close small gaps, and make slightly crooked teeth look straighter.

How long do veneers last?

Veneers are very durable. With good care they can last many years, potentially decades.

What is the difference between porcelain and composite veneers?

Both are tooth-colored. Porcelain is more durable and resists stains; composite is matched to your tooth color but is less durable and may not last as long.

Are dental veneers painful to get?

No. Veneers are usually placed under local anesthesia, so you shouldn't feel pain during the procedure.
