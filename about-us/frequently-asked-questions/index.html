Your visit

What type of dentistry do you provide?

We offer a wide range of care: dental implants, restorations like crowns and veneers, and general dentistry. Our office has several advanced treatment options, so most problems can be handled here. See the full list of services.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring a photo ID and any dental records or X-rays you have. Please also have your personal details ready, such as your Social Security number and your family health history.

Do you accept walk-in or same-day patients?

Yes. We see same-day patients for dental emergencies. Walk-ins are welcome too, though you may have to wait. Bring a photo ID and plan to fill out paperwork while you wait. Calling ahead at 954-998-1252 is the fastest way to be seen.

How do I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Just call us at 954-998-1252. It's the quickest and easiest way to change your appointment.

Payment & insurance

What types of payment do you accept?

We accept debit cards, credit cards and checks. You can also pay over time with financing, with options for no, bad or average credit. Applying takes about 30 seconds and doesn't affect your credit score. See financing.

Do you accept insurance plans?

We accept most PPO dental insurance plans, and we'll check your benefits for you before treatment. [CONFIRM: the current live page says no insurance, Medicare or Medicaid is accepted. Confirm PPO acceptance and whether Medicare/Medicaid are accepted.]

What if I don't have insurance?

You can still afford care. Uninsured patients can join our Kleer membership plan ([PLAN COST]), which brings a single implant, abutment and crown to $1,789. We'll also help you find a financing plan that fits your budget. Your consultation and 3D scan are free.
