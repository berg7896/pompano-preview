Dr. Tatiana Herzog, DDS

Board-certified implantologist

Dr. Herzog was one of the first board-certified implantologists in South Florida [CONFIRM: "one of the first" claim]. She graduated from the Misch Implant Institute and is a Diplomate of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists. She has been with Implant Center of Miami since 2010, bringing her implant expertise to patients across South Florida.

In 2014 she founded Implant Instructors, an advanced training program that teaches other dentists how to plan implant treatment, explain it clearly to patients, and run a surgical practice. She hand-picked its faculty and trains doctors locally and internationally. She is known as a leader in implant dentistry and has traveled around the world to discuss implant theory with other experts in the field.

A message from Dr. Herzog: why I became a dentist

I started college as an accounting major, and I wasn't excited about it. When I tried to graduate early, my advisor wouldn't sign off: I hadn't taken a single elective outside my major. So I signed up for jewelry making, expecting to string beads. Instead we were carving wax, casting gold and setting stones, and I was struggling.

That weekend, antiquing in Miami Beach with my mother, I found a box of sharp little tools. They were dental instruments. The seller wanted a quarter each; I paid $5 for the whole box. I brought them to class, fell in love with carving wax, and made a lot of jewelry that semester.

That's when I knew I was meant to work with my hands, and that dentistry, with those very same instruments, would never feel like work.

Dr. Jennifer Schaumberg, DDS

Board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon

Dr. Schaumberg grew up in West Bloomfield, Michigan, and earned both her undergraduate and dental degrees at the University of Michigan. She completed her internship and residency at the University of Miami / Jackson Memorial Hospital, a major center for craniofacial trauma, tumor and reconstructive surgery.

Her advanced training covers tooth extractions and other dentoalveolar surgery, corrective jaw (orthognathic) surgery, dental implants, facial cosmetic surgery, tumor and reconstructive surgery, pre-prosthetic surgery and craniofacial trauma. She is licensed and trained in all levels of anesthesia, holds privileges at Holy Cross Hospital and takes emergency call there. She also offers Botox, Dysport and injectable fillers.

Teaching

Has led courses and clinics at the Broward County Dental Research Clinic, lectures to students at Nova Southeastern University Dental School, and teaches continuing education on lasers, CT scans, implants, bone grafting, cosmetic surgery, pathology and reconstruction.

Giving back

Volunteers with Dentistry from the Heart, the Broward County Dental Clinic and Holocaust Survivor Donated Dental Services.

Mentoring and leadership

Founded and leads the local Women's Dental Study Group. Mentors students through the Pine Crest School Summer Externship Program, the Alpha Omega International Fraternity externship program and Nova Southeastern, and welcomes young people interested in medicine and dentistry to observe in the office. Serves on the Board of the Florida Society of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery as chair of its Hospital and Insurance Committee.

Outside the office

The daughter of a dentist and an educator, she is married with three children, whom she calls her greatest accomplishment. She regularly takes courses to keep up with new surgical and anesthesia technology, and aims to treat every patient like family.
