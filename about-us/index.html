About us

We focus on doing a few things very well: dental implants, veneers, crowns, Invisalign and emergency care. Two board-certified doctors and our own dental lab, all under one roof in Pompano Beach.

Serving Broward County & beyond since 2010

Striving for dental excellence in Boca Raton, Fort Lauderdale & Lighthouse Point

Our doctors offer a wide range of care: dental implants to replace missing teeth, restorative work like crowns and bridges, and general dentistry. Our modern office has several advanced treatment options, so most patients can get everything they need in one place instead of being sent across town.

We are part of Implant Center of Miami, with sister offices in Bay Harbor Islands, Coral Gables, Sunrise and Coral Springs.
