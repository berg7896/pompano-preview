A patient story

From failing teeth to a full smile, starting in one day

With All-on-4, the surgery and your first set of fixed teeth happen in the same visit, here in our office. Here is how the whole journey usually goes.

  1. Consult and 3D scanFree. You leave knowing exactly what you need and what it costs.
  2. Surgery dayExtractions and implants under sedation, and you leave with fixed temporary teeth.
  3. HealingOver a few months the implants fuse with your jawbone.
  4. Your final teethCustom-made in our own lab and fitted by your doctor.
