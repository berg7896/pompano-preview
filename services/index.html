Frequently asked questions

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Usually you need good oral health and enough jawbone. If you have gum disease or thin bone, treatments such as bone grafts or sinus lifts can often make implants possible. Your free consult and 3D scan will tell you.

What dental procedures can improve the appearance of my smile?

Teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, enamel shaping and gum contouring can all improve your smile. We'll suggest what fits your goals.

How long does a dental implant procedure take?

It varies. Some cases allow same-day placement; others take several months over a few stages while the implant heals.

Can I whiten my teeth if I have dental restorations?

Whitening works on natural enamel, not on crowns or veneers. We may suggest replacing restorations afterward, or another cosmetic option, so everything matches.

Are dental implants painful?

Implants are placed under local anesthesia, with sedation available. Soreness afterward is managed with medication, and most patients report minimal pain.
